Kolluru Venkata Kiran

Empire AI Postdoctoral Research Fellow

City University of New York

I am a postdoctoral researcher, and my research lies at the interface of physics, mathematics, and biology, with a particular emphasis on nonlinear dynamics and statistical-physics approaches to turbulent and turbulence-like phenomena. A recurring theme in my work is understanding how large-scale structures emerge in nonlinear chaotic systems, ranging from fully developed (inertial) turbulence to turbulence in low–Reynolds-number active suspensions, as well as in weakly nonlinear dispersive partial differential equations (PDEs). I combine large-scale GPU simulations with analytical work on the underlying hydrodynamical PDEs.
